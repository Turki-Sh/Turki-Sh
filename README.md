### ⚙️ Engineering Focus & Projects

**HARIS | Computer Vision & Threat Recognition**

Led a team of five developing a multi-stage CCTV threat-recognition system combining RT-DETR, BoT-SORT, RTMPose, and ST-GCN. Achieved 0.745 end-to-end F1 on UCF-Crime and reduced per-frame weapon false positives from 8.49% to 0.75%.

[Case Study](https://turkialshuaibi.com/work/haris/) · [Project Website](https://turki-sh.github.io/HARIS/)

**Sarjy | Bilingual Voice AI Assistant**

Independently built a conversational voice assistant supporting English and Saudi Arabic, with persistent memory, external tool integration, and real-time speech interaction. Built with Next.js, Groq Whisper, and Orpheus.

[Case Study](https://turkialshuaibi.com/work/sarjy/) · [Live Application](https://sarjy-three.vercel.app/)

**Onkith | Privacy-Preserving AI**

Deputy lead of a six-person team developing a privacy-first voice assistant. Built speech processing components and personally identifiable information (PII) masking capabilities covering 33 entity types.

[Case Study](https://turkialshuaibi.com/work/onkith/) · [Project Website](https://onkith.online/)

**Madaric | AI Career Intelligence**

Independently developed a bilingual career platform combining CV parsing, candidate assessment, opportunity matching, and Saudi job-market insights.

[Case Study](https://turkialshuaibi.com/work/madaric/) · [Live Application](https://madaric.site/)

**Foreman | Open-Source Agentic Development**

Created an eight-stage development playbook for AI coding agents, enforcing requirements gathering, design decisions, implementation checks, and deployment verification.

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Turki-Sh/Foreman) · [Documentation](https://foreman.turkialshuaibi.com/)
